Our Story

Founded in Cape Town in 1997 by Dr Iqbal Survé, Sekunjalo grew from a seed of capital into a multinational with interests across seven sectors of the economy.

The Group was established to promote broad-based economic participation and transformation in South Africa — one of the country's largest black-owned investment groups, operating on a philosophy of long-term value creation, empowerment and sustainable growth.
